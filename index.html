ABOUT

About Me

Hello, I'm Juchan Son, a Ph.D. student in Library and Information Science at Yonsei University.

My path runs from electronic engineering through theology (M.Div., Th.M.) to library and information science. That trajectory is not accidental: it grows out of the conviction that the one-to-one comparison at the heart of traditional biblical scholarship can be pushed further with computational-linguistic methods.

I analyze large-scale ancient Greek corpora (TLG, the Perseus Digital Library, and GLAUx) with text-mining techniques to visualize the relationships between the New Testament and its contemporary literature, and to bring fresh perspectives to established interpretations.

PROJECT 01 In Progress

Tracing Semantic Change in New Testament Vocabulary

신약성서 어휘 의미 변화 추적

Extracting usages of the same word across contemporary literature from the 8th c. BCE to the 4th c. CE, to quantitatively analyze the semantic spectrum and shifts of key New Testament vocabulary.

Python TLG Word2Vec

Character Network Analysis across Corpora
